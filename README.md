# Societal Innovation Collaboration Portal

> A digital platform connecting real-world societal challenges with Higher Education Institutions (HEIs), faculty, students, industry, and government for collaborative problem solving.

## Overview

The Societal Innovation Collaboration Portal is an AI-assisted platform designed to bridge the gap between **societal problems and academic/industry capabilities**.

Citizens, community organizations, and government bodies can submit real-world challenges. The platform analyzes each challenge, identifies its domain and priority, finds relevant HEIs and faculty, and enables universities and industry partners to collaborate on solving the problem.

The complete lifecycle is managed through the platform:

**Challenge → AI Analysis → HEI Matching → Proposal → Project → Faculty/Students → Industry Collaboration → Milestones → Government Monitoring**

The project is designed around the Smart India Hackathon problem context and supports the goals of experiential learning, research, innovation, industry collaboration, and community engagement.

---

## Problem

Societal problems often exist at the local level but do not have a structured mechanism to reach institutions capable of solving them.

At the same time:

- Universities have research expertise and infrastructure but may not know which local problems need attention.
- Students and faculty need meaningful real-world projects.
- Industry can provide mentorship, technology, funding, prototyping, and deployment support.
- Government needs visibility into ongoing initiatives and their impact.

This creates a disconnect between:

**Problem → Expertise → Collaboration → Implementation → Impact**

Our platform aims to close this gap.

---

## Solution

The platform creates a structured collaboration ecosystem where:

1. Citizens or organizations submit societal challenges.
2. AI analyzes and categorizes the challenge.
3. A priority score is generated based on severity, urgency, affected population, and geographic impact.
4. Similar challenges can be detected.
5. Relevant HEIs are recommended based on domain expertise, faculty availability, infrastructure, and location.
6. Relevant faculty members are recommended.
7. HEIs can submit solution proposals.
8. Approved proposals become active projects.
9. Faculty and students can participate in projects.
10. Industry can collaborate through mentorship, funding, prototyping, testing, or pilot support.
11. Projects are tracked through milestones.
12. Government can monitor challenges, projects, participation, and district-level activity.

## Core Features

### AI-Assisted Challenge Analysis

The platform analyzes submitted challenges to provide:

- Challenge category
- Priority score
- AI confidence
- Duplicate/similarity detection

Current supported domains include:

- Agriculture
- Healthcare
- Education
- Water Management
- Environment

### Intelligent HEI Matching

HEIs are ranked using multiple factors:

- Domain relevance
- Institutional expertise
- Faculty availability
- Infrastructure
- Geographic relevance

Location intelligence considers the district of the challenge and the district of the HEI.

### Faculty Matching

Faculty members are recommended based on:

- Domain expertise
- Expertise level
- Availability
- Department
- HEI association
